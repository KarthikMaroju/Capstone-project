Delivered

deck (77% missing) dropped; age (20%) median-imputed (28.00); embarked/embark_town (0.2%) rows dropped. Cleaned shape (889,14) confirmed.

A3-6Univariate, bivariate, multivariate, z-score▼
Expected

Age/fare histograms+boxplots+IQR outliers. 6×6 correlation matrix + heatmap. ≥4 multivariate charts with interpretations. EDA-only z-score sanity check.

Delivered

B7-10Split, preprocessing, 3 classifiers, evaluation▼
